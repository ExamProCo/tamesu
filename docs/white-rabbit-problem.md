# The White Rabbit Problem

![Alice following the White Rabbit](the-white-rabbit-problem.jpg)

I want to solve what I call the White Rabbit Problem.

An agent workload appears close enough to working that developers keep chasing it, but
each iteration takes them deeper into complexity without making feasibility any clearer.

The prototype looks 70% complete. The remaining 30% becomes an expanding research
project. Eventually, the developer runs out of runway without knowing whether success was
days or months away. They have no clear idea of the cost, the feasibility, or how to carry
the project forward within their organization.

Tamesu is meant to make that work measurable. It keeps a record of what changed, what
improved, what failed, and what each attempt cost. The goal is to give developers enough
evidence to decide whether to continue, narrow the problem, or stop.

The [Tamesu approach](approach.md) explains how that works.
